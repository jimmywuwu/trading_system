# Crypto Structural Inefficiency Research Roadmap

> Jaquan note: 目前幾個公開 OI / funding 實驗沒有找到乾淨 alpha，這不是壞事。比較真實的判讀是：我們找到的大多是 **market state / risk information**，不是可直接提款的方向性 alpha。下一步應該把研究路線從「單一公開指標預測方向」轉向「cross-venue friction、forced-flow timing、risk-regime edge」。

## Executive summary

目前結論：

- 單一 venue 的 OI / funding / leverage-pressure 指標，較適合作為：
	- risk classifier
	- volatility regime filter
	- confidence / sizing modifier
	- avoid-bad-entry filter
- 還不支持作為：
	- standalone directional alpha
	- Trader handoff
	- production strategy
- 未來 alpha 若存在，較可能出現在：
	- 跨 venue 的資金/槓桿摩擦
	- forced-flow 的時序差
	- spot / perp / options / ETF flow 的錯配
	- 低流動性時段的脆弱性

## Research operating principle

每個 milestone 都要輸出階段性研究結論，不追漂亮 equity curve。

結論分級：

```yaml
Reject: no stable effect, benchmark/control dominates, or data invalid
Research Only: mechanism plausible, evidence weak/mixed, useful as classifier
Risk Filter Candidate: improves drawdown/volatility/entry quality, not directional alpha
Paper Candidate: out-of-sample and controls reasonable, costs/slippage plausible
Production Candidate: rare; requires robust walk-forward, execution, risk, paper evidence
```

## Milestone 1 — Finish leverage-state classifier family

**Goal:** Convert failed/weak alpha findings into a reliable risk-regime classifier.

### Research questions

- Does true deleveraging reduce future cascade risk?
- Does passive notional OI shrink leave hidden leverage fuel?
- Does price-down + raw-OI-up predict higher volatility, continuation, or squeeze risk?
- Are effects different for BTC vs ETH?

### Candidate features

```yaml
true_deleverage:
  price_down: true
  notional_oi_down: true
  raw_oi_down: true

passive_notional_shrink:
  price_down: true
  notional_oi_down: true
  raw_oi_flat_or_up: true

sticky_or_short_build:
  price_down: true
  raw_oi_up: true

risk_on_leverage_build:
  price_up: true
  raw_oi_up: true
  notional_oi_up: true
```

### Tests

- Rolling 90d percentile thresholds instead of fixed thresholds.
- Matched controls:
	- recent volatility
	- recent return
	- UTC hour
	- funding regime
- Outcomes:
	- 24h / 48h / 72h realized volatility
	- max adverse excursion
	- tail frequency
	- rebound quality
	- directional return only as secondary.

## Milestone 2 — Cross-venue funding and OI dispersion

**Goal:** Test whether structural fragmentation contains more information than single-venue OI/funding.

### Mechanism

Crypto leverage is fragmented across venues. If Binance / Bybit / OKX / Hyperliquid funding or OI diverge, that may reveal local forced positioning, constrained arbitrage capital, or venue-specific stress.

### Research questions

- Does cross-venue funding dispersion predict convergence, volatility, or tail events?
- Are local funding extremes more useful when other venues do not confirm?
- Does venue-specific OI stress spill over to BTC/ETH global volatility?

### Data needs

- Binance perp funding/OI
- Bybit funding/OI
- OKX swap funding/OI
- Hyperliquid funding/OI if historical access is feasible
- mark/index premium where available
- common BTC/ETH price series

### Tests

- Dispersion z-score.
- Highest-vs-lowest venue funding spread.
- OI share migration.
- Venue disagreement regimes:
	- all venues crowded long
	- one venue crowded long
	- offshore vs onchain-perp divergence
	- funding dispersion high but price flat

### Deliverable

- Cross-venue feasibility/data quality report.
- Event study: dispersion vs forward vol/tail/convergence.

### Gate

Promote only if cross-venue effect survives matched volatility/return controls.

## Milestone 3 — Spot-led vs perp-led move classifier

**Goal:** Distinguish real demand/supply from levered price movement.

### Mechanism

Spot-led moves may reflect real capital flow. Perp-led moves may reflect leveraged positioning that is more vulnerable to unwind.

### Research questions

- Do spot-led rallies have better continuation than perp-led rallies?
- Do perp-led selloffs have higher reversal/squeeze risk or higher continuation risk?
- Does spot confirmation improve leverage-pressure signal quality?

### Features

- spot volume impulse
- perp volume impulse
- perp OI change
- funding change
- mark/index premium
- spot/perp volume ratio
- basis movement

### Tests

Classify moves into:

```yaml
spot_led_up
perp_led_up
spot_led_down
perp_led_down
mixed_confirmation
no_confirmation
```

Outcomes:

- forward return
- forward realized vol
- drawdown after entry
- trend continuation probability
- failed breakout probability

### Deliverable

- Spot/perp leadership report.
- Candidate feature contract for future strategy/risk layer.

## Milestone 4 — Weekend / low-liquidity fragility

**Goal:** Test whether the same leverage pressure is more dangerous during thin liquidity windows.

### Mechanism

Crypto trades 24/7, but market-maker coverage, inventory appetite, and depth are not constant. Equal leverage pressure may be more dangerous on weekends, late UTC hours, or low-volume windows.

### Research questions

- Does leverage pressure have larger tail risk on weekends?
- Do low-volume / low-depth windows amplify OI/funding stress?
- Can this become a risk throttle for strategies?

### Data needs

- UTC hour / weekend flag
- volume
- spread/depth if available
- OI/funding pressure
- forward tail returns

### Deliverable

- Low-liquidity fragility event study.
- Decision: risk throttle candidate or reject.

## Milestone 5 — Options expiry / dealer hedge unwind

**Goal:** Study whether options calendar events explain perp OI changes and short-horizon volatility better than perp-only metrics.

### Mechanism

Options expiry can cause dealer hedge unwind, which may show up as perp OI changes without a classic liquidation cascade.

### Research questions

- Do monthly/weekly options expiries systematically alter perp OI?
- Are OI drops around expiry different from liquidation-driven OI drops?
- Does options expiry interact with failed breakout / pinning / gamma regimes?

### Data needs

- Deribit BTC/ETH options OI by expiry/strike/call-put
- options expiry calendar
- perp OI/funding from major venues
- price and realized vol

### Deliverable

- Options-expiry / perp-hedge-unwind study.
- Classification of expiry-driven vs liquidation-driven OI contraction.

## Milestone 6 — Stablecoin and ETF liquidity pulse

**Goal:** Test slower structural liquidity flow rather than high-frequency perp signals.

### Mechanism

Stablecoin supply and ETF flows may represent buying power / capital allocation, while perp funding represents levered positioning. Their mismatch may be more informative than either alone.

### Research questions

- Do stablecoin supply changes lead crypto beta regimes?
- Does ETF inflow + weak perp positioning create healthier rallies?
- Does ETF outflow + high leverage create fragility?

### Data needs

- stablecoin supply, preferably by issuer/chain
- exchange stablecoin balances/inflows if source quality acceptable
- BTC/ETH ETF daily flows
- BTC/ETH perp OI/funding

### Deliverable

- Liquidity pulse regime report.
- Candidate macro/liquidity filter.
